Science Spotlight 🌌

🛸✨ Space Science · NASA

Voyager 1 Is Now a Whole LIGHT-DAY Away From Earth! 🤯🌠

NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft just hit a mind-blowing milestone 🚀 — it's now so incredibly far away that it takes a full 24 hours for light to travel from Earth to it! That distance is called a "light-day"! 💡 Remember — light travels at 300,000 km every single second and it still takes a whole day! Voyager 1 launched in 1977 (before your grandparents were adults!) and is the most distant human-made object ever built. It has now left our solar system entirely 🌌 and is drifting through the space between stars! NASA is celebrating with a special event on November 17 called "Shine Bright for Voyager's Light-Day" ⭐ — go outside and look up at the stars that night! 🌠🔭
